    <h2 class="text-2xl font-bold text-gray-100 mb-6">How I'm Approaching Each Area</h2>
    <div class="space-y-4">

      <div class="bg-gray-800 rounded-xl p-5 border-l-4 border-red-500">
        <h3 class="text-white font-bold mb-1">Iteration &amp; Loop Tracing</h3>
        <p class="text-gray-400 text-sm leading-relaxed">
          This gets day one because it's my biggest gap. My approach is to trace loops manually using a step-by-step table
          instead of running through them in my head. I'll work through all three AP CSP loop types and spend extra time on
          robot grid navigation questions, which require the same careful sequential thinking.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="bg-gray-800 rounded-xl p-5 border-l-4 border-orange-400">
        <h3 class="text-white font-bold mb-1">Math Expressions &amp; Modulo</h3>
        <p class="text-gray-400 text-sm leading-relaxed">
          I'm keeping this simple on purpose. My plan is to memorize the three modulo patterns that appear most on AP CSP —
          even/odd checks, wrap-around cycling, and last-digit extraction — so I can recognize them instantly instead of
          re-deriving them under pressure.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="bg-gray-800 rounded-xl p-5 border-l-4 border-orange-400">
        <h3 class="text-white font-bold mb-1">Random Values &amp; Algorithm Development</h3>
        <p class="text-gray-400 text-sm leading-relaxed">
          Two things I'm locking in: that <code class="bg-gray-700 px-1 rounded text-orange-300">RANDOM(a, b)</code> is inclusive on both ends (different from Python),
          and a consistent method for tracing unfamiliar algorithms — identify inputs, process, and output before answering.
          I'll practice this on problems I haven't seen before.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="bg-gray-800 rounded-xl p-5 border-l-4 border-yellow-400">
        <h3 class="text-white font-bold mb-1">Distributed &amp; Parallel Computing</h3>
        <p class="text-gray-400 text-sm leading-relaxed">
          I understand the concepts — it's the calculation questions where I drop points. I'm drilling the speedup model:
          sequential time = sum of all tasks, parallel time = longest single task, dependent tasks can't be parallelized.
          A handful of focused problems will lock this in.
        </p>
      </div>
