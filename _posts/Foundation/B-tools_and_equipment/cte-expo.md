# CTE Expo

**Date:** May 21

**Location:** DNHS Quad

**Extra Credit Theme:** What you saw and what you learned?

## Proof of Experience

Image was uploaded in the interactive version. Add the image file to your blog repository and link it here.

**Caption:**  
This is a first responders club at Mount Carmel where they teach fire school and how to become a first responder.

## Before the Event

I expected a bunch of tables with pamphlets and people trying to recruit volunteers.

## Curiosity Before Attending

I was curious whether any of the organizations would actually be doing something relevant to tech or computer science.

## What I Saw

I visited booths from organizations focused on environmental sustainability, youth mentorship, food insecurity, and community health.

## Most Interesting Thing I Saw

The most interesting one was a group using data tracking to help athletes improve.

## What I Learned

I learned that a lot of nonprofits are starting to use simple data tools to identify where their help is needed most.

## What Surprised Me

It surprised me that something as basic as a spreadsheet could have that big of an impact on resource distribution.

## What Inspired Me

The data tracking project with UCI inspired me the most because it was such a cool project that interested me.

## Personal Connection

It connected to my interest in using data and tech to solve real problems, which is something I want to do in healthcare or engineering down the line.

## Interaction

**Person or group I learned from:** Coordinator

I talked to a volunteer coordinator from the community health booth. She explained how they track patient outreach across different zip codes and use the results to plan where to send mobile clinics.

## Depth of Experience

It changed how I think about what CS can actually look like outside of a classroom or startup.

## Connection to CS or Future Goals

It connects directly to my interest in applying tech to healthcare and to projects I've worked on involving data.

## Final Reflection

The event showed me that nonprofits are quietly doing some of the most meaningful tech-driven work out there.

## Biggest Takeaway

> you don't need a big company to use data to make a real difference.
