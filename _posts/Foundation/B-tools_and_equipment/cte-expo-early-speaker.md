## Before the Event

I expected a very intellectual presentation because of his suit and slideshow.

## Curiosity Before Attending

I was curious about his company and what he did.

## What I Saw

I saw a great slideshow prepared. Elijah was also wearing a suit which made him seem very professional.

## Most Interesting Thing I Saw

The most interesting thing that I saw was his football pictures because I love football. He achieved this at a very high level and he said it taught him a lot about business.

## What I Learned

One specific thing I learned was that I should be myself when pitching my product. The people who are investing in my business are investing in me more than they are the product. That is why it is super important to be yourself.

## What Surprised Me

Something that surprised me was the way that he started the company. He had a company before this but there was a competition that only new companies could enter so he brainstormed an idea to make voters feel more confident that their vote matters.

## What Inspired Me

The thing that inspired me the most was how he got his friends in on the business. He knew nothing about the technical aspect of this business so he asked his friends for help and that is what brought it to the next level.

## Personal Connection

This connected to my own interests because I am always trying to work on new projects that can further advancement and I never thought of asking my friends to help me out.

## Interaction

**Person or group I learned from:** Presenter Elijah Johnson

They explained how he was able to take his business to the next levels through their connections despite not knowing how to get there orginally.

## Depth of Experience

This event changed my thinking because he told me that no dream is too big despite not having the background knowledge. Elijah Johnson knew nothing about blockchain originally but he got their through hard work. He also never knew he would be in the type of job that he would have to wear a suit to work but he got to that point.

## Connection to CS or Future Goals

This connects to my future projects because all I need is that one person to beleive in me to get my project started. If I believe in it, I should pitch the project to everyone I know. I need to envision the project being successful if I ever want it to be.

## Final Reflection

This event was meaningful to me because it really opened my eyes in the world of business. I have always dreamed of starting a successful business and I have a ton of ideas but I feel helpless when starting it. I feel like a small highschooler in a huge world. Now, I will start before I am ready.

## Biggest Takeaway

> My biggest takeaway was starting before I am ready.
